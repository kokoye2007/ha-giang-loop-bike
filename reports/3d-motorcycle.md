# 3D Motorcycle Scroll Study

The “Meet the ride” workshop sits between the trip summary and itinerary. It renders a bundled 255 KB generic road-sportbike GLB rather than the original block-built illustration. The model is CC0, unbranded and lightweight enough for the static site.

Scrolling now moves the motorcycle through a restrained 100-degree inspection arc rather than a full spin. Scale grows from 1.25 to 2.4 around the middle, then eases back. Reverse scrolling reverses the same motion. Rendering occurs only after scroll, resize or theme changes rather than in a permanent animation loop.

The canvas is decorative but has an accessible label. Reduced-motion users receive a fixed three-quarter view and a shorter section. If the pinned Three.js module or WebGL cannot load, a static motorcycle mark and clear fallback message remain visible.

The public research desk no longer lists Australia-specific Smartraveller, Klook Australia or unrelated New Zealand Intrepid references. Currency planning remains in AUD; the removal applies to reference links and country-specific riding copy.
